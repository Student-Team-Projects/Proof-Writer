=== RULES ===
1. **Format and syntax**
   - Output must be **valid JSON only** (no text outside JSON).

   - Each "statement" must contain **only one logical move** (no multi-step jumps).

3. **Simplicity and minimality**
   - Avoid redundant or circular steps — include only what is logically required.

4. **Dependencies**
   - Each `depends_on` list must reference only earlier IDs.
   - No forward references or cyclic dependencies.

5. **Structure of reasoning**
   - If the proof uses induction, include separate steps for:
     - base case,
     - inductive hypothesis,

     - the derivation of contradiction,

   - Substitution of equals for equals.
   - Logical implication (modus ponens).
   - Definition-based rewriting.

   - Arithmetic and set-theoretic axioms (as appropriate).

7. **Conclusion**
   - The last step of the proof must have a statement logically equivalent to the given `{STATEMENT_HERE}`.
   - The `"conclusion"` field must repeat this statement exactly.

   }
